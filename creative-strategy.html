FAQs

Is this for individuals or businesses?

Both. I work with musicians, artists, creators, founders and creative businesses. The strategy is customized to what you’re building, whether that’s an artist career, a personal brand, a product, a service or a growing business.

What if I don’t have a traditional “brand”?

You don’t need one. We can work from wherever you are, whether you’re developing your first creative project, refining an existing business or trying to make sense of a brand that’s grown organically.

Is this marketing consulting or brand strategy?

A bit of both. We start with the bigger picture: what you’re building, who it’s for, what makes it different and how you want to be perceived. From there, we translate that strategy into practical marketing and content decisions.

How is this different from a Strategy Session?

A Strategy Session is two 60-minute calls to answer one question, and you apply the answer yourself. The Intensive is a 90-day partnership: I research your market before every session, we cover your positioning, brand, audience and marketing, and I support you between sessions. If you want answers, start with a Strategy Session. If you want a partner, this is the one. Book the Intensive within 14 days of a Strategy Session and your $400 is credited.

Is this the same as hiring an agency?

No. This is a strategy-first engagement where you remain the person executing the work. I’m there to provide the research, strategic thinking, direction and accountability. If you need a team to execute the strategy for you, that’s something we can discuss separately.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

That’s okay. You don’t need to come in with a perfectly defined problem. Part of the first phase is figuring out what’s actually getting in the way and where your biggest strategic opportunities are.

How much work happens between sessions?

You’ll have focused action items between sessions, but this isn’t designed to become another full-time job. I’ll give you the priorities that matter most so you know where to put your energy.
