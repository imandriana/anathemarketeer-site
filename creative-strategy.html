Is this you? FAQ

Do I need to already have a brand or strategy figured out?

Nope. That’s literally what we’re here to figure out. You can come in with a fully formed project, a messy collection of ideas, or somewhere in between. We’ll turn the chaos into a clear strategic direction.

Is this only for musicians and artists?

No. Musicians and artists are some of the creatives I work with most, but the program is also designed for creators, entrepreneurs, and other creative projects that need help with positioning, branding, and marketing.

What if I don’t know exactly what I need help with?

That’s okay. You don’t need to arrive with the perfect question. We’ll identify the biggest strategic gaps together and use each session to move the project forward.

How much time will I need to dedicate between sessions?

After each session you’ll get a short, tailored set of action items and exercises to move your project forward before we meet again. I keep it focused so it fits around your real life, and you can message me with quick questions in between.

Is this different from booking a 1:1 strategy session?

Yes. A 1:1 strategy session solves one specific problem. The Creative Strategy Intensive builds the bigger strategy over time, with research, recaps and action plans in between. It’s not just 6 consulting calls: each session builds on the last.

What if I need implementation help too?

This program is strategy-first. If you need implementation, content creation, or a larger marketing team, I can recommend the next step or explore a custom engagement with you.
