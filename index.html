Hi, I'm Ana. I'm obsessed with why people care about things.

I’ve spent the last 8+ years figuring out what makes brands click: the stories they tell, the people they attract, and the strategy underneath it all. Now I help founders, creatives, and businesses turn that thinking into brands and marketing people actually care about.

Read my story →

My first love was broadcast journalism. At 19, I landed my own show at my college radio station. It was called Bear Trap, it played electronic trap music, and my biggest fan (my mom) tuned in religiously. It was my first public brand, built long before I knew what branding meant.

From there came a music blog, integrating into Toronto’s music scene, an internship at MuchMusic (Canada’s MTV), and eventually a pivot into marketing in 2018, when it became clear the media industry had approximately 0.01% job security. Since then, I’ve worked with brands including Boston Pizza, Yoga International, and Purity Woods. In 2023, I founded my own agency, Phlare Marketing.

Then in 2025, Ana The Marketeer came to life: my own platform for decoding branding and marketing strategy and making it actually useful. Basically, I’ve been trying to figure out why people care about things for a very long time.
