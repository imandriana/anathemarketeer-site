Hi, I'm Ana. I'm obsessed with why people care about things.

Marketing, culture and storytelling have been the same obsession since I was 19. I want to know why one brand becomes a cult favourite and another gets scrolled past.

Read my story →

My first love was broadcast journalism. At 19, I landed my own show at my college radio station. It was called Bear Trap, it played electronic trap music, and my mom tuned in religiously. It was my first public brand, built before I knew what branding meant.

From there: a music blog, Toronto's music scene, an internship at MuchMusic (Canada's MTV), and a pivot into marketing in 2018, once it was clear the media industry had approximately 0.01% job security.

I've since worked with brands like Cineplex, Revlon, Boston Pizza and Yoga International, and in 2023 I founded my own agency, Phlare Marketing. In 2025, Ana the Marketeer came to life: my own brand, built to decode marketing and branding strategy so you can actually use it.
